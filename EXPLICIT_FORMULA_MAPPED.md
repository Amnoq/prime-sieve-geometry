# The Explicit Formula, Mapped to the Circle Sieve

*Mapped 2026-10-11, at his direction ("map the equation that derives RH
to the circle sieve"). Each term of the von Mangoldt explicit formula
mapped to its circle-sieve correspondent: what it conventionally is,
what is smuggled in through its terminology, and its mark — **DERIVED**
(earned relationally), **INSERTED** (installed by the equation form),
**OPEN** (no correspondent established — not invented). The equation is
balanced; the mapping shows what the balancing imports.*

The equation:

    ψ(x) = x − Σ_ρ (x^ρ/ρ) − log(2π) − ½log(1−x⁻²)

---

## Left side

**ψ(x)** — "the primes"
- Conventional: sum of log p over prime powers p^k ≤ x.
- Smuggled: prime powers and logarithmic weights. The word says
  "primes"; the mathematics uses ψ. The multiplicative structure —
  part of what is to be explained — is installed before the equation
  begins.
- Circle-sieve map: **INSERTED** (terminology). Correspondent:
  composites mapped first-class, each showing everything that went
  into making it. The left side is already a mapped object, not raw
  primes.

**x** — the main term
- Conventional: the smooth count; the "expected" drift beneath the
  oscillation.
- Smuggled: smoothness itself — the assumption that counting has a
  smooth drift underneath, against which the zeros oscillate.
- Circle-sieve map: **OPEN**. Candidate: the linear trace of the
  seed's propagation — the line on which relation is mapped. Not
  established; not invented.

## Right side

**Σ_ρ (x^ρ/ρ)** — "the zeros"
- Conventional: sum over the non-trivial zeros of the continued zeta.
- Smuggled, itemized:
  1. *Analytic continuation.* ρ are zeros of the extension, not of
     Euler's sum, which never had zeros. The continuation — the whole
     functional-equation apparatus — is installed before the first
     zero is named.
  2. *"Non-trivial."* The terminology encodes the functional equation's
     sine factor. The trivial zeros are artifacts of the equation's
     form, not features of the primes.
  3. *The summation.* Σ_ρ does not converge as written. It requires
     conjugate pairing and ordering by imaginary height — a summation
     convention installed by hand; the pairing (ρ ↔ 1−ρ̄) leans on
     the functional equation a second time.
  4. *Hadamard's product.* Summing over zeros at all requires the
     factorization ξ(s) = ξ(0)∏(1−s/ρ) — installed 1893.
  5. *x^ρ.* Two characters carrying branch cuts and the full
     complex-analytic apparatus.
- Circle-sieve map: **DERIVED** (the zetas themselves — tones measured
  from prime orbits, relationally earned, not posited) but **INSERTED**
  (their placement — the continuation, the summation, the product).
  Derived content, inserted form, coexisting in one term.

**log(2π)** — the constant
- Conventional: the archimedean contribution.
- Smuggled: the "place at infinity" — treated as just another prime
  and folded into a constant. A whole place smuggled through
  terminology.
- Circle-sieve map: **OPEN**.

**½log(1−x⁻²)** — the trivial-zero correction
- Conventional: contribution of the trivial zeros.
- Smuggled: the trivial zeros are artifacts of the functional
  equation's form — this term is the equation correcting for its own
  machinery.
- Circle-sieve map: **INSERTED** (artifact of form, not of relation).

## The "="

- Conventional: identity.
- Smuggled: the bridge itself. Asserts the prime side and the zero
  side are the same thing — installs the relation by balancing.
  Both sides are constructed objects; the equation balances
  constructions, not observations.
- Circle-sieve map: **INSERTED**. The Circle Sieve's alternative: a
  relational bridge of *constructions* between nodes that only relate
  to zero — built node to node, not installed by balancing.

---

## What the mapped equation shows

1. The formula is an assembly: derived content (the zetas) carried
   inside inserted form (the continuation, the summation convention,
   the prime-powers-called-primes, the "=").
2. Nothing here invents a correspondent where none is established
   (x, log(2π): OPEN — the honest marks).
3. The work ahead, in these terms: construct the bridge the "="
   installs — node to node, between zero-relating nodes — and watch
   which mapped terms dissolve (artifacts of form) and which survive
   (relations). The 0.5 balance point, seen from the two orderings
   without any equation, is the first candidate for what survives.

## Conclusion (worked forward 2026-10-11, at his direction: "proceed until you hit a conclusion")

Starting from tonight's premises — 0 as undifferentiated ground, 1 as
base unit and last number, 2 as first marked node, primes as nodes
relating only to zero, composites mapped first-class, the two orderings
(generative 0→1→2; linear 2→…→1), 0.5 as the balance point — the mapped
equation resolves into a sort:

**SURVIVES (derived, relational):**
- The zetas — tones measured from prime orbits, not posited.
- Prime-marking — nodes where the undifferentiated zero is the only
  thing to relate to. Marking discovers; it does not predicate.
- Composites — mapped first-class, everything that went into making
  them. Nothing eliminated, invariably.
- The linear trace — the line (and circle) on which relation is mapped.
  The map, not the ground.
- The 0.5 balance point — seen from the two orderings held together,
  no equation required.

**DISSOLVES (inserted, artifacts of the equation form):**
- The "=" — the installed bridge. To be replaced by construction,
  node to node.
- The smooth/oscillatory split — x as baseline. A process has no
  baseline; it propagates and marks. The split is the equation's
  requirement (something smooth for the zeros to correct), not the
  process's. Note the precision: the *trace* (linearity) survives;
  the *baseline* (smooth drift) dissolves.
- ψ-terminology — prime powers called "primes."
- Analytic continuation, summation convention, Hadamard product —
  the machinery that makes "the zeros" nameable inside an equation.
- Trivial zeros and their correction — the equation correcting for
  its own form.
- log(2π) — the archimedean place folded into a constant.

**The inversion this yields:** the formula needs the 0.5; the 0.5 does
not need the formula. The critical line is where the two orderings
balance — the equation installs what balance already shows. RH, in
these terms, is the conjecture that the installed bridge coincides
with the constructed one: that the zeros the equation sums are the
tones the orbits derive, all standing at the balance point.

**Honest grade:** this is a philosophical conclusion, not a
mathematical one. It proves nothing about RH. What it does — and this
is what "map, not proof" requires — is give the relational program a
precise account, in its own terms, of what the explicit formula *is*:
an assembly of derived content in inserted form. The mathematical work
— constructing the bridge node to node — remains open. The conclusion
is derived-conditional on tonight's framework, which is heuristic.
